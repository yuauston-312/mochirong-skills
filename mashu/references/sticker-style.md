# Mochirong 麻薯小人表情包筛选风格

Use this reference when collecting or filtering sticker assets.

## Identity

The target sticker family is `mochirong`, also seen as `Mochirong麻薯小人`, `麻薯小人`, or `Mochirong 麻薯小人无字表情包`. Treat generic `mochi`, `mochi cat`, `kawaii ghost`, and food mochi stickers as off-target unless they visually match Mochirong very closely.

## Target Look

- White bean, dumpling, or soft ghost-like body.
- Simple black outline, hand-drawn doodle style.
- Minimal face: dot eyes, tiny mouth, blank stare, deadpan, silly reaction, or slight blush.
- Mostly white, gray, transparent, or very light background.
- Square-ish sticker framing, suitable for chat reactions.
- Everyday reaction poses: awkward, happy, stunned, eating, dancing, patting, sleepy, confused, smug, speechless.
- Some stickers may include small Korean/Chinese/Japanese reaction text, but wordless stickers are preferred.

## Strong Matches

- Search result or source explicitly says `mochirong`.
- Search result or source says `Mochirong麻薯小人`.
- White rounded line-art character with the same tiny face and noodle-like limbs as the user-provided references.
- Low-detail meme-style white bean character with black line art.

## Reject

- Generic anime characters.
- Cats, bears, Snoopy, brand mascots, food mochi, or unrelated animals.
- Halloween-only ghosts that look scary rather than like Mochirong.
- Complex illustrations with many colors.
- Real photos.
- 3D rendered stickers.
- Generic `cute ghost` results unless they are nearly identical to Mochirong.
