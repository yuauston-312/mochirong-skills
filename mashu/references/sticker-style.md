# Mashimaro Sticker Screening Standard

Use this reference when collecting or filtering sticker assets.

## Identity

The target sticker family is `Mashimaro`, also known as `MashiMaro`, `마시마로`, `Yeopgi Tokki`, `엽기토끼`, or Chinese `流氓兔` / `麻薯君`. Treat `Mochirong`, generic white blob characters, mochi food stickers, cute ghosts, Molang, Miffy, cats, bears, and generic line-art people as off-target.

## Required Look

- South Korean Mashimaro rabbit IP.
- White, plump rabbit body with long rabbit ears.
- Simple black outline and minimal facial features.
- Usually deadpan, sleepy, mischievous, or absurdly calm.
- Chat-sticker framing, preferably transparent, white, or light background.

## Strong Matches

- Source title, filename, page text, or metadata explicitly says `Mashimaro`, `MashiMaro`, `마시마로`, `Yeopgi Tokki`, `엽기토끼`, `流氓兔`, or `麻薯君`.
- Visual identity clearly shows Mashimaro's white rabbit character, not only a generic white creature.

## Reject

- Any `Mochirong` / `麻薯小人` / `薯门` soft-blob character.
- Generic white ghosts, dumplings, blobs, or line-art people.
- Animals that are not Mashimaro, including generic rabbits.
- Anime characters, photos, 3D renders, plush-only product shots, food, logos, or unrelated memes.
- Any image whose source or visual identity is ambiguous.
